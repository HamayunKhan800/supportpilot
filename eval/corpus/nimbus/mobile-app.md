# Nimbus on your phone and tablet

## Requirements

The Nimbus mobile app works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later, and on Android 10 or later. Download it from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with your Nimbus account.

## Browse and open files

The app shows your whole Nimbus space. Files aren't stored on your phone until you open them, so the app uses very little space. Tap a file to preview it; documents, photos, videos and PDFs open right in the app.

## Files offline

To keep a file or folder on your phone, tap the three dots and choose **Make available offline**. Offline files update automatically when you're connected. Find them all under **Offline** in the menu.

## Upload from your phone

Tap **+** to upload photos, take a picture, scan a document to PDF, or create a folder. Mobile uploads are limited to 2 GB per file.

## Passcode lock

Turn on **Settings > Passcode** to require Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or a 4-digit passcode when you open Nimbus. This keeps your files private if someone else uses your phone.

## Lost phone

Sign out the lost device remotely from the web app under **Settings > Security > Devices**. Offline files on the device are removed the next time it connects.
