# Share files with a link

A sharing link lets anyone with the link open a file or folder, even if they don't have a Nimbus account.

## Create a link

1. Right-click a file or folder and choose **Share**, or select it in the web app and click **Share**.
2. Click **Create link**.
3. Choose what people can do: **Can view** lets them see and download; **Can edit** lets people with a Nimbus account change the file.
4. Click **Copy link** and send it however you like.

## Link settings

Paid plans can add extra protection under **Link settings**:

- **Expiry date:** the link stops working after the date you choose. Available on Personal, Family and Business.
- **Password:** people must enter a password before the file opens. Available on Personal, Family and Business.
- **Disable downloads:** people can preview the file but not download it. Available on Business only.

On the Free plan, links have no expiry date and no password, so only share them with people you trust.

## Stop sharing

To turn off a link, open **Share**, click the link and choose **Delete link**. It stops working immediately for everyone. Creating a new link gives a different address, so the old one never works again.

You can see all your active links under **Settings > Sharing > Links**.

## Business sharing rules

Business administrators can restrict links, for example to allow links only for people inside the company, or to require an expiry date on every link.

## How many people opened my link?

On Personal, Family and Business plans, click a link in **Settings > Sharing > Links** to see how many times it was viewed and downloaded. Viewer names aren't shown for people without a Nimbus account.
