# Delete your Nimbus account

Deleting your account removes your files and your personal information from Nimbus. Before you start, download anything you want to keep, for example with a data export.

## Before you delete

- **Cancel any paid plan first.** If you pay through the App Store or Google Play, cancel the subscription there as well, because deleting your account does not stop the store from charging you.
- **Family organizers:** delete or transfer the Family plan first. Members get a notice and keep their files on a Free plan.
- **Business members:** your account belongs to your company. Ask your administrator to remove you.

## How to delete

Go to **Settings > Account > Delete account**, enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

## The 30-day grace period

Your account is deactivated immediately: you're signed out everywhere, links stop working and shared folders are no longer available to others. For **30 days** you can still change your mind: sign in and click **Restore my account**, and everything comes back as it was.

After 30 days, your files are permanently erased from our servers and cannot be recovered by anyone, including our support team. Backups are overwritten within a further 35 days.
