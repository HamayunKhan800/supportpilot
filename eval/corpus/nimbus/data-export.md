# Export a copy of your data

You can download a copy of everything Nimbus stores about you: your files, their folder structure, your account details and your activity history. This is your right under data-protection laws such as the GDPR.

## Request an export

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy > Export my data**.
2. Choose what to include: files, account information, or both.
3. Click **Request export**.

We prepare the export in the background. Large accounts can take a while: exports are ready **within 48 hours**, and usually much sooner. We email you when it's ready.

## Download it

The email contains a download link. For your security, the link **expires after 7 days** and asks for your password. The export is a ZIP archive; very large exports are split into several ZIP files of up to 50 GB each.

Account information comes as JSON files that any program can read. Shared folders owned by other people aren't included, because those files belong to them.

If you only need a few folders, it's quicker to download them from the web app: select them and click **Download**.
