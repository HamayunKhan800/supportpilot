# Getting started with Nimbus

Nimbus Cloud Storage keeps your files safe in the cloud and in sync across your computer, phone and the web. This guide walks you through your first ten minutes.

## Create your account

Sign up at app.nimbus.example with your email address, or use "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple". Every new account starts on the Free plan with 15 GB of storage. No payment card is needed to sign up.

We send a confirmation email straight away. Click the link in it within 24 hours to verify your address. Until you verify, you can upload files but you cannot share them with other people.

## Install the apps

- **Desktop:** download the Nimbus app for Windows, macOS or Linux from nimbus.example/download. After you sign in, it creates a folder called "Nimbus" in your home folder. Anything you put in that folder is uploaded automatically.
- **Mobile:** get Nimbus from the App Store or Google Play to see your files on the go and back up your photos.
- **Web:** open app.nimbus.example in any supported browser. Nothing to install.

## Add your first files

Drag files into the Nimbus folder on your computer, or click **Upload** in the web app. A green tick on a file means it is safely stored in the cloud. A blue arrow means it is still uploading.

## Next steps

- Turn on two-step verification to protect your account.
- Share a folder with a colleague or family member.
- Turn on camera uploads in the mobile app so your photos are backed up automatically.
