# Payment methods and failed payments

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. In some countries we also accept iDEAL, SEPA Direct Debit and Apple Pay in the web app.

Business teams with **10 or more users on a yearly plan** can pay by invoice with a bank transfer. Contact sales to switch to invoicing.

## Update your card

Go to **Settings > Billing > Payment method** and click **Update**. The new card is used for your next payment. We never store your full card number: payments are processed by our payment provider, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.

## If a payment fails

Payments can fail when a card expires, has insufficient funds or is blocked by the bank. When that happens:

1. We email you right away and show a banner in the app.
2. We retry the payment automatically **3 times over 7 days**.
3. If all retries fail, your account moves to the Free plan. Your files are kept, but if you use more than 15 GB, syncing stops until you pay or free up space.

To fix it sooner, update your payment method and click **Retry payment** under **Settings > Billing**.
