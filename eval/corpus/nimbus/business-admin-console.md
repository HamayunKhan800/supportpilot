# The Business admin console

The admin console is where Business administrators manage the team. Open it from your profile menu and choose **Admin console**. Only team owners and administrators can see it.

## Add and remove users

- **Invite users:** go to **Members > Invite** and enter their work email addresses. Each new user adds a licence to your bill.
- **Suspend a user:** they can't sign in, but their files and shared folders stay available. Suspended users still use a licence.
- **Remove a user:** choose what happens to their files. You can transfer them to another member, which is recommended when someone leaves the company, or delete them after 30 days.

## Transfer files from a departing employee

When you remove a user, pick **Transfer files to** and choose a member. That person receives a folder named after the departing user, with all their files and their shared folder ownership. Sharing links created by the departing user keep working.

## Help members with sign-in problems

Administrators can't see anyone's password. But they can:

- send a password reset email to a member,
- **reset two-step verification** for a member who lost their phone, so they can set it up again,
- sign a member out of all their devices, for example when a laptop is stolen.

## Audit log

The audit log records sign-ins, sharing changes, file deletions, admin actions and more. Filter it by person, action or date, and export it as CSV. Audit log events are kept for **1 year**.

## Storage and activity reports

The Reports page shows how much storage each member uses, how many files were shared outside the company, and which devices are connected. Reports update once a day.

## Roles

A team has one owner, who manages billing and can delete the team. The owner can make other members administrators. Administrators can do everything except change billing and delete the team.
