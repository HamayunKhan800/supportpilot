# Cancel your subscription

You can cancel a paid plan at any time. There are no cancellation fees.

## How to cancel

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel plan** and tell us why (optional).
3. Confirm. We email you a confirmation.

If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel in your phone's subscription settings instead; you can't cancel those subscriptions from Nimbus.

## What happens next

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. On that date your account moves to the **Free plan** with 15 GB of storage. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period; see our refund policy for when refunds apply.

## If you use more than 15 GB

Nothing is deleted. Your files stay in your account and you can still view, share and download them. But your account becomes **over quota**: syncing and new uploads stop until you either delete files to get under 15 GB or choose a paid plan again.

Features of paid plans stop working: password-protected links lose their password and expire, and older file versions beyond 30 days are removed.

## Changed your mind?

Before the end of your paid period, click **Keep my plan** under **Settings > Billing** to undo the cancellation.
