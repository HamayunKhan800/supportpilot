# Refund policy

We want you to be happy with Nimbus. Here's when you can get your money back.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full **within 30 days of purchase** or renewal. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can cancel so that the plan doesn't renew.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, you keep your paid features until the end of the month you've paid for.

## Charged by mistake

If you were charged twice, or charged after you cancelled, contact support with the date and amount. We refund duplicate or mistaken charges in full, whatever plan you're on.

## How long refunds take

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We send them on the same day we approve them, but your bank can take **5 to 10 business days** to show the money in your account. PayPal refunds usually appear within 3 days.

## App Store and Google Play purchases

If you subscribed in the Nimbus iPhone or Android app, Apple or Google handled the payment, so we can't refund it ourselves. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or from Google Play under **Payments & subscriptions**.

## How to ask for a refund

Go to **Settings > Billing**, find the payment and click **Request refund**, or contact support. Refunds for Business plans are handled by the account owner.
