# Upgrade or downgrade your plan

## Upgrading

Upgrades take effect **immediately**. You pay only the difference for the rest of your current billing period (we prorate the price), and from then on the new price applies. Extra storage is available as soon as the payment goes through.

To upgrade, go to **Settings > Billing > Change plan** and pick a plan.

## Downgrading

Downgrades take effect **at the end of your current billing period**, so you keep what you paid for. Before you downgrade to a plan with less storage, make sure your files fit; otherwise your account will be over quota and syncing stops until you free up space.

## Switching between monthly and yearly

Switching from monthly to yearly happens right away, and the unused part of your month is credited. Switching from yearly to monthly happens when your year ends.

## Moving from Personal to Family

Upgrading from Personal to Family keeps all your files. You become the Family organizer and can invite up to 5 more people.
