# Shared folders

A shared folder lets you work on the same files with other people. Everyone you invite sees the folder in their own Nimbus space, and changes sync to everyone.

## Invite people

1. Right-click a folder and choose **Share**.
2. Enter the email addresses of the people you want to invite.
3. Choose a role for each person:
   - **Viewer:** can open and download files.
   - **Editor:** can add, change and delete files, and invite others if you allow it.
   - **Owner:** there's one owner, who controls who has access. You can make someone else the owner.
4. Click **Invite**. People without a Nimbus account are asked to create a free one.

## Storage

A shared folder counts only toward the **owner's** storage. If you join a 100 GB shared folder, it doesn't use any of your own space.

## Leave or remove

- To leave a folder someone shared with you, right-click it and choose **Leave folder**. Your copy disappears, but the folder stays for everyone else.
- The owner can remove people under **Share > Manage access**. Removed people lose access right away; files they already downloaded to their computer stay there as normal files that no longer sync.

## Nested sharing

You can't share a folder that's inside another shared folder with different people. Share the parent folder instead, or move the folder out.
