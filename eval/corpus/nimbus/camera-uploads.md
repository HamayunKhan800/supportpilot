# Back up photos with camera uploads

Camera uploads copy every photo and video you take to Nimbus automatically, so you won't lose them if your phone is lost or broken.

## Turn it on

In the mobile app, go to **Settings > Camera uploads** and turn it on. Choose whether to include videos. The first backup can take a long time if you have many photos; keep the app open and the phone charging to speed it up.

Photos are saved in a folder called **Camera Uploads**, named by the date and time they were taken.

## Wi-Fi and mobile data

By default, camera uploads only run on **Wi-Fi**, to save your data plan. To upload over mobile data too, turn on **Use cellular data**. Videos still wait for Wi-Fi unless you also turn on **Upload videos over cellular**.

## Background uploads

On iPhone, iOS limits background activity, so uploads may pause until you open Nimbus again. Turning on Background App Refresh and Location access helps uploads run more often. On Android, allow Nimbus to run without battery optimisation.

## Photo formats

iPhones save photos as HEIC. Nimbus keeps the original HEIC file by default. Turn on **Save HEIC photos as JPG** if you need to open them on devices that don't support HEIC. Live Photos are saved as a photo plus a short video.

## Duplicates

Nimbus recognises photos it has already uploaded and skips them, even if you reinstall the app.
