# Install the desktop app

The Nimbus desktop app keeps a folder on your computer in sync with your Nimbus account.

## System requirements

- **Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- **macOS:** macOS 12 Monterey or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs.
- **Linux:** Ubuntu 22.04 or later, Debian 12, and Fedora 39 or later. We provide .deb and .rpm packages.
- At least 2 GB of free disk space for the app and its cache, plus space for any files you keep on the device.

## Install it

1. Download the installer from nimbus.example/download.
2. Run it. On a Mac, drag Nimbus into the Applications folder and open it. On Windows you don't need administrator rights; the app installs for your user only.
3. Sign in. Choose where to put your Nimbus folder, or keep the default location in your home folder.
4. Pick which folders to sync, or sync everything.

On macOS, Nimbus asks for permission to show files in Finder. Allow it under **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders**, or files won't appear.

## Updates

The app updates itself in the background. We support each version for 12 months; older versions stop syncing and ask you to update.

## Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your files from Nimbus. Files you kept on the computer stay in the Nimbus folder as normal files.
