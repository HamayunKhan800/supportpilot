# Student discount

Students get **50% off the Personal plan** on yearly billing, which is $49.50 per year instead of $99.

## Who can get it

You need to be enrolled at a university, college or other higher-education school. High-school students aren't eligible, but can use the Family plan of a parent.

## How to apply

1. Go to nimbus.example/students and click **Verify student status**.
2. Our partner SheerID checks your enrolment. Most students are verified instantly with their school email address; others upload a student ID or enrolment letter.
3. Once you're verified, pick the yearly Personal plan and the discount is applied at checkout.

## Renewal

The discount lasts as long as you're a student. We ask you to verify again **every 12 months**, before your renewal. If you're no longer a student, your plan renews at the normal Personal price; you can cancel or switch plans before then.

The student discount can't be combined with other offers, and it isn't available for the monthly Personal plan or for Family and Business plans.
