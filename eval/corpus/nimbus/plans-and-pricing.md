# Plans and pricing

Nimbus has four plans. Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax or VAT, which is added at checkout based on your country.

## Free

- 15 GB of storage
- Desktop, mobile and web apps
- File version history for 30 days
- Sharing links that never expire

## Personal

- 2 TB of storage for one person
- $9.99 per month, or $99 per year (two months free)
- Version history for 180 days
- Sharing links with passwords and expiry dates
- Nimbus Vault for end-to-end encrypted files
- Email support

## Family

- 2 TB of storage shared by up to 6 people
- $14.99 per month, or $149 per year
- Each member has a private space; the organizer manages the plan
- Everything in Personal

## Business

- 5 TB of storage per user, pooled across the team
- $15 per user per month billed annually, or $18 per user billed monthly
- Minimum of 3 users
- Admin console, single sign-on (SAML), SCIM provisioning and audit log
- Version history for 365 days
- 24/7 chat and phone support

## Paying yearly

Annual plans are paid up front for 12 months and cost less than paying monthly. You can switch from monthly to yearly billing at any time; the remaining value of your current month is credited.

## Non-profits and education

Registered non-profit organisations get 30% off Business. Students can get a discount on Personal; see the student discount article.
