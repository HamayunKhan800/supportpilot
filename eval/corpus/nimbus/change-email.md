# Change the email address on your account

You can change the email address you use to sign in to Nimbus at any time.

1. Go to **Settings > Account > Email address** and click **Change**.
2. Enter your new address and your current password.
3. We send a confirmation link to the **new** address, and a notice to your **old** address. Click the link within 24 hours to finish the change.

Until you confirm, you keep signing in with your old address. The notice to your old address includes a link to cancel the change, in case someone else requested it.

## Things to know

- The new address can't already be used by another Nimbus account. If it is, delete or change that other account first.
- Shared folders and links keep working; people you share with will see your new address.
- Your invoices from now on show the new address. Past invoices don't change.
- **Business accounts:** your email address is managed by your company's administrator. Ask them to change it in the admin console.
