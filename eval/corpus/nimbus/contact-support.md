# Contact Nimbus support

## How to reach us

Click **Help** in the app or go to help.nimbus.example and click **Contact support**. Sign in first, so we can see your plan and help you faster.

## Support by plan

- **Free:** the help centre and the community forum. Our team answers account and billing questions by email.
- **Personal and Family:** email support, with a reply within **24 hours**, 7 days a week.
- **Business:** 24/7 live chat and phone support. Urgent issues, such as nobody on the team being able to access files, get a response within **1 hour**.

Support is available in English, German, French and Spanish.

## Is Nimbus down?

Check status.nimbus.example for the current status of every Nimbus service and any ongoing incidents. You can subscribe there to get emails or text messages when there's a problem and when it's fixed.

## Beware of scams

Nimbus support will never ask for your password, your two-step verification codes or your backup codes. We'll never ask you to install remote-access software. Our emails only come from addresses ending in @nimbus.example.
