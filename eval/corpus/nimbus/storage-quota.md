# What counts toward your storage

Your storage quota is the amount of space your plan includes: 15 GB on Free, 2 TB on Personal, 2 TB shared on Family, and 5 TB per user on Business.

## What counts

- Every file in your Nimbus space, including files inside folders you've shared with others.
- **Files in the trash.** Deleted files keep using space until they're permanently removed. Empty the trash to free up space right away.
- Files in your Nimbus Vault.

## What doesn't count

- **Older versions** of your files. Version history is free.
- Folders other people share with you. They count toward the **owner's** storage, not yours.
- Files people upload to you through a file request count toward your storage, because you own them once they arrive.

## Check your usage

Go to **Settings > Storage** to see how much space you use and what uses it most. The **Largest files** view helps you find big videos and old backups to clean up.

## When you run out of space

When your storage is full, new files stop uploading and the desktop app shows the error NC-507. Existing files stay safe and you can still view and download them. Free up space or upgrade to start syncing again.
