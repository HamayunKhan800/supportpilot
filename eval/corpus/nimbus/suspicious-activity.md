# Suspicious sign-ins and security alerts

## New sign-in emails

We email you whenever your account is used on a new device or browser. The email shows the device, the approximate location and the time. If it was you, there's nothing to do.

## If you don't recognise a sign-in

Act quickly:

1. Click **This wasn't me** in the email, or go to **Settings > Security > Devices** and click **Sign out** next to the unknown device.
2. Change your password right away. Pick one you don't use anywhere else.
3. Turn on two-step verification if it isn't on yet.
4. Check **Settings > Sharing > Links** and **Settings > Connected apps** for links or apps you didn't create, and remove them.
5. Check your trash and file activity for files that were deleted or changed, and restore them if needed.

## Phishing emails

Scammers sometimes send fake emails that look like they're from Nimbus, asking you to "verify your account" or saying "your storage is full". Real Nimbus emails only come from @nimbus.example and never ask for your password in the email. If you're unsure, don't click the link: open app.nimbus.example yourself.

Forward suspicious emails to phishing@nimbus.example.

## Temporary locks

After 10 wrong password attempts in a row, we lock sign-in for that account for 15 minutes to stop people guessing. You can still reset your password during this time.
