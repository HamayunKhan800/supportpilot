# Reset your password

If you forgot your password, you can set a new one from the sign-in page.

## How to reset it

1. Go to app.nimbus.example and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you use for Nimbus.
3. Open the email from Nimbus and click **Reset password**. The link works once and expires after 60 minutes.
4. Choose a new password. It must be at least 12 characters long. Your other devices are signed out for safety, so you will need to sign in again on them.

## I didn't get the reset email

- Check your spam or junk folder, and search for mail from no-reply@nimbus.example.
- Make sure you typed the address you signed up with. For security, we don't tell you whether an address has an account.
- Wait a few minutes. You can request a new link after 5 minutes; requesting a new link cancels the old one.

## Accounts that use single sign-on

If your company signs you in through single sign-on (SSO), your password is managed by your company, not by Nimbus. The reset link won't work for these accounts. Contact your IT team instead.

## Signed in with Google or Apple?

If you created your account with "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", you don't have a Nimbus password. Keep using that button to sign in, or add a password under **Settings > Security**.
