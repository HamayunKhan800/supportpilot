# Using Nimbus in your browser

The Nimbus web app at app.nimbus.example lets you use your files on any computer without installing anything.

## Supported browsers

We support the latest two versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari and Microsoft Edge. Other browsers may work, but we don't test them. JavaScript and cookies must be enabled.

## Upload files and folders

Click **Upload** and pick files, or drag files and whole folders from your computer into the browser window. Uploads through the browser are limited to 5 GB per file; use the desktop app for larger files. Keep the tab open until uploads finish.

## Preview and edit

The web app previews more than 150 file types, including Office documents, PDFs, images, videos and code. To edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, click **Open in** and choose Microsoft 365 or Google Docs.

## Using a public or shared computer

If you use Nimbus on a computer that isn't yours, untick **Keep me signed in** when you sign in, and sign out when you're done. You can sign out of all browsers at once under **Settings > Security > Devices**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? in the web app to see all keyboard shortcuts. For example, / jumps to search and Shift+U opens the upload dialog.
