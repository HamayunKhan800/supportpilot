# Recover deleted files

When you delete a file or folder, it goes to the trash first, so you can get it back if you change your mind.

## How long deleted files are kept

- **Free plan:** 30 days in the trash
- **Paid plans:** 90 days in the trash

After that, files are permanently deleted. Files in the trash still count toward your storage.

## Restore from the trash

1. Open the web app and click **Trash** in the sidebar.
2. Select the files or folders you want back. Use the search box or sort by deletion date to find them.
3. Click **Restore**. Files go back to their original folder, or to the top of your Nimbus space if that folder no longer exists.

Deleting a file on one device deletes it everywhere, so restoring from the web app brings it back on all your devices too.

## Empty the trash

Click **Empty trash** to permanently delete everything in it right away and free up space. This can't be undone, and support can't recover files after the trash is emptied.

## Files deleted by someone else

If someone deletes a file in a folder you share, it goes to their trash and yours. Business administrators can also restore files that a team member deleted, for up to 90 days, from the admin console.
