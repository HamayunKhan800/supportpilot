# Recover a locked account

Use account recovery if you can't sign in and the usual fixes haven't worked: you lost your two-step verification device and your backup codes, or you no longer have access to the email address on your account.

## Start a recovery request

1. On the sign-in page, click **Can't sign in?** and then **Recover my account**.
2. Enter the email address on the account and, if you still can, the address you'd like to use from now on.
3. Answer the verification questions. We may ask about recent files, devices you used, or the last four digits of the payment card on a paid plan.

## The 72-hour waiting period

To protect you from someone pretending to be you, every recovery request has a **72-hour waiting period** before access is restored. During this time we email every address and notify every device linked to the account. If the request wasn't made by you, click **This wasn't me** in that email to cancel it straight away.

Our support team **cannot skip or shorten** the waiting period, even if you contact us. This is deliberate: it's what keeps attackers out.

## After recovery

When the waiting period ends, you'll get an email with a link to set a new password. Two-step verification is turned off, so turn it on again right away and save new backup codes.

Business accounts are recovered by your company's Nimbus administrator, not through this form.
