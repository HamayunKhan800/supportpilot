# Two-step verification

Two-step verification (also called two-factor authentication or 2FA) asks for a second proof that it's you each time you sign in on a new device. Even if someone learns your password, they can't get into your account without it.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-step verification** and click **Turn on**.
2. Choose a method:
   - **Authenticator app** such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password. Scan the QR code and enter the 6-digit code it shows.
   - **Security key** such as a YubiKey, or a passkey on your phone or computer.
3. Save your **backup codes**. You get 10 one-time codes. Store them somewhere safe, like a password manager or a printed copy.

We don't offer codes by text message (SMS), because SMS can be intercepted through SIM swapping.

## Signing in

After you enter your password on a new device or browser, Nimbus asks for a code from your app or a tap on your security key. You can tick "Trust this device for 30 days" on your own computer.

## Lost your phone or security key?

Sign in with one of your backup codes instead of the 6-digit code. Each code works only once. Once you're in, set up two-step verification again on your new device and generate fresh backup codes.

If you have no backup codes left, follow the account recovery process. Business users can ask their administrator to reset two-step verification for them.
