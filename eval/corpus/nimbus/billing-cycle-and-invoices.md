# Your billing cycle and invoices

## When you're charged

Your billing date is the day you first subscribed. Monthly plans renew on the same day each month, and yearly plans on the same date each year. If your billing date doesn't exist in a month (for example the 31st), you're charged on the last day of that month.

We email a reminder 7 days before every yearly renewal, so you have time to change or cancel your plan.

## Find your invoices

Every payment creates an invoice. We email it to you, and you can download all past invoices as PDF files under **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.

## Company details and VAT

To show your company name, address or VAT number on invoices, add them under **Settings > Billing > Billing details** before your next payment. Customers in the EU who enter a valid VAT ID are charged without VAT under the reverse-charge rules.

Invoices can't be edited after they are issued, because tax law requires them to stay unchanged. If an invoice has a mistake, contact support: we'll issue a credit note and a corrected invoice.

## Currency

Prices are shown and charged in US dollars, euros or British pounds depending on your billing country. Your bank may add a fee if your card is in a different currency.
