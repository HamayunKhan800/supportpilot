# The Family plan

The Family plan gives up to 6 people (the organizer plus 5 members) 2 TB of storage to share.

## Private by default

Each member gets their own private Nimbus space. Members can't see each other's files unless they share them. The organizer pays for the plan and manages who is in it, but **cannot see members' files** either.

## Shared storage

The 2 TB is one pool for the whole family. The organizer can see how much each person uses and can set a storage limit per member under **Settings > Family**.

## Invite someone

The organizer goes to **Settings > Family > Invite member** and enters an email address. The invitation is valid for 14 days. Members must be at least 13 years old (16 in some EU countries). A person can belong to only one Family plan at a time.

If the invited person already has a paid Personal plan, they should cancel it first; they'll get a prorated refund for the unused time.

## Removing a member or leaving

When a member leaves or is removed, their files stay with them. They move to the Free plan with 15 GB and have **30 days** to delete files or upgrade if they use more than that, before syncing stops.
