# Refund exceptions for loyal customers

Customers who have been with Nimbus for more than two years may qualify for a goodwill refund outside the normal 30-day window. Contact support and mention how long you've been a customer.

## Processing exceptions

Goodwill refunds are reviewed by the billing team within 5 business days.
